# Product Requirements Document

## Problem
Most platforms either give charts, screening, or trading execution. This project combines research, AI, alerts, paper trading, and backtesting in one explainable system.

## Users
- Beginner retail investors
- Swing traders
- Long-term investors
- Quant researchers
- Market analysts
- Students learning technical analysis

## Core use cases
- Find bullish or bearish setups
- Scan stocks using rules
- View live charts and indicators
- Simulate paper trades
- Test strategies historically
- Track risk and performance
- Understand market regime

## Success metrics
- Accurate and explainable alerts
- High-quality paper trading simulation
- Backtest realism
- Fast chart interaction
- Reliable data ingestion
- Clear checkpoint continuity
