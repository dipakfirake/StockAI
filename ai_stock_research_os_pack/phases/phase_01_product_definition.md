# Phase 1 — Product Definition

## Goal
Define exactly what the platform is, who it is for, and what it will not do.

## Deliverables
- Problem statement
- User personas
- Core use cases
- Non-goals
- MVP scope
- Success metrics

## Questions to answer
- Who is the first user?
- What is the first valuable action?
- What must exist on day one?
- What should be postponed?
- What makes this platform different?

## Exit criteria
- Product scope is clear
- MVP is defined
- Phase 2 can begin
